读课本 Reading Plus（p.75），点 🔊 听原句。每句旁边的圆圈数字 = 它走的是哪一步。找一找，是不是都在路线图里？

Reading Plus · p.75 · Making New Friends at School
①Do you like to make new friends at school?
①Here's some of my advice.
②First, don't be shy!
②Try to talk to all of your classmates.
②Smile and say, "Hello! What's your name?"
②Then give your name and say, "Nice to meet you!"
②It's important to get people's names right.
③Second, ask questions!
③Ask about school, sport, music, and more!
③As you listen, think of more questions to ask.
③But remember, you have to be a good listener before you can ask good questions.
④Last, don't forget to share!
④Tell your classmates something interesting about yourself.
④Then your new friends can get to know you too!
④Soon enough, you'll be the best of friends!
⑤I hope you find my advice helpful!
⑤Now go make some new friends!
